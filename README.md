# The Nightmare Before Christmas

## Overview
**The Nightmare Before Christmas** is a 1993 stop-motion musical fantasy film produced by *Tim Burton* and directed by Henry Selick. It follows Jack Skellington, the Pumpkin King of Halloween Town, who discovers Christmas Town and tries to celebrate Christmas in his own spooky way.

> "Just because I cannot see it, doesn't mean I can't believe it!"

## Basic Info
- **Release Year:** 1993
- **Director:** Henry Selick
- **Music:** Danny Elfman
- **Genre:** Fantasy / Musical

## Main Characters
1. **Jack Skellington** — The Pumpkin King
2. **Sally** — The clever ragdoll
3. **Oogie Boogie** — The villainous boogeyman
4. **Zero** — Jack's ghost dog
5. **Santa Claus** — The Christmas icon

## What Makes It Great
- Blends Halloween and Christmas
- Memorable musical soundtrack
- Timeless stop-motion animation
- Heartwarming story about identity
